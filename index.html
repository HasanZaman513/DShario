DShario User Guide

A simple guide to creating, sharing and managing your DShario.

1. Create your cardAdd the professional and contact information you want to use. Keep optional details blank when you do not need them.
2. Choose a share typeChoose Business, Career, Networking, Social or Full. The selected share type determines what information the card will carry.
3. Choose how to shareMy DShario Link is your permanent card address. Temporary Share lasts 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days and can be revoked early.
4. Add PDFs to Temporary ShareAdd an optional message and upload up to 2 PDFs, maximum 2 MB each. The message and PDFs stay available only while the Temporary Share is valid. Expiry or revocation stops future DShario access, but cannot remove a copy already downloaded by the recipient.
5. Share, QR and downloadSend a link or QR. You can also download a digital-card image, choose a card template, and use its QR to return to the live DShario card.
6. Save a received DSharioFrom a permanent or valid Temporary Share, choose Save to My Connections. If sign-in is required, DShario returns to the received card and completes the save. Once saved, View in My Connections becomes the single navigation action and opens your saved connections directly.
7. Exchange with my DSharioA DShario member signs in and is taken to My Card → Share Card, where the same five Share Types are used. People who do not use DShario can use the separate contact-return option when it is available.
8. My Connections and historyOne sender appears as one active connection. Saving a newer view from the same sender makes it the current saved card. Earlier cards remain only as a lightweight history trace, without keeping the old card details. Received and Saved date/time help you track when the connection happened.
9. Temporary Share expiry and revocationWhen a Temporary Share expires or is revoked, its live card actions and temporary details are no longer available. A searchable history trace may remain. If the same sender is later saved through a permanent link, that permanent card becomes the active connection.
10. Organise professional connectionsIn My Connections you can search for people, mark favourites, add a category, record how you connected, keep a private note and set a follow-up. Use Manage for detailed controls and history. Notes can be edited or deleted without deleting the connection.

DShario Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

DShario is currently a prototype digital identity and contact-sharing service. This policy describes the information handled by the current prototype and how it is used.

1. Information we handle

Depending on the features you use, DShario may handle account and authentication information; card information such as name, title, organisation, introduction, location, website, phone, email, WhatsApp, social links, profile image and card appearance; privacy-view and share-link settings; temporary-share tokens, expiry, revocation and optional message information; PDF documents you choose to upload for Temporary Share; DShario connections you choose to save; connection-view status used to show a simple “DShario updated” indicator; and contact details voluntarily submitted through Contact Exchange.

2. Why we use information

We use this information to authenticate users, create and store cards, apply selected privacy views, generate permanent and temporary sharing experiences, provide QR and digital-card downloads, provide Temporary Share messages and documents, save user-requested DShario connections, show a simple update indicator when a saved user changes their card, deliver contact exchanges to the intended card owner, maintain basic service security and operate and improve the prototype.

3. Sharing and recipient access

DShario is designed for user-directed sharing. A card owner chooses what information to enter, which privacy view to use and whether to create a permanent or temporary share. Recipients may be able to view, copy, save, screenshot or forward information that has been shared with them. A temporary link's expiry or revocation prevents future access through DShario but cannot recall information or documents already downloaded or copied by a recipient.

4. My Connections

Signed-in DShario users may choose to save another DShario user in My Connections. A saved permanent connection can point to the sender’s current DShario information for the saved privacy view. DShario may indicate that the card has been updated since it was last viewed, without providing a full change history. A Temporary Share connection may remain identifiable after expiry or revocation, but the temporary message, documents and temporary-share access are no longer available through DShario.

5. Contact Exchange and Received Contacts

When a recipient chooses to send contact details back, those details are provided to the relevant card owner and stored in the owner's Received Contacts area subject to prototype limits. The owner can save a contact to their phone and delete it from DShario. DShario does not currently treat Received Contacts as a permanent address book.

6. Temporary messages and documents

An optional sender message and PDF documents are available only through Temporary Share. The message may appear as an initial popup and remains readable on the receiver interface while that Temporary Share is valid. PDF documents are optional and are available only through Temporary Share. Current prototype limits are displayed in the interface. Access through DShario follows the associated temporary link's validity. Users should avoid uploading highly sensitive documents and should understand that a recipient may retain a downloaded copy.

6. Service providers and storage

DShario currently uses Supabase for authentication and backend database/storage functions and uses website-hosting infrastructure to deliver the application. These providers may process technical and account/service information as necessary to provide their services. QR generation in the current DShario build is performed within the application rather than by sending the card URL to a separate QR-code service.

7. Overseas processing

Service providers may process or store information outside Australia or the user's country. Because DShario remains a prototype and its production infrastructure is not final, we do not currently promise a particular data-residency location. This section should be updated when production hosting and data locations are finalised.

8. Security

DShario uses access controls appropriate to the current prototype, including authenticated ownership controls and database access policies. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Use a strong unique password, choose privacy views carefully and avoid sharing highly sensitive information through a digital card.

9. Access, correction and deletion

Users can sign in to review and update their card, delete their card, and delete received contacts from the DShario inbox. Some account or backend information may require assistance to access or delete. Contact DShario through its official LinkedIn Page until a dedicated privacy contact is established. Deletion from DShario does not delete copies previously saved by another person.

10. Retention

Card and account-related information is retained while needed to provide the service or until deleted where the prototype provides deletion controls, subject to legitimate operational, security or legal needs. Temporary-share access follows its expiry or revocation status. Received Contacts is intended to be temporary rather than a permanent contact archive. Retention rules should be made more specific before commercial launch.

11. Language

DShario currently provides its interface and card labels in English. User-entered names, titles, companies, links and other content are displayed as entered.

12. Children and sensitive information

DShario is intended for professional and adult networking and is not designed for children. Do not use card fields or document sharing for passwords, payment credentials, government identifiers, health information or other highly sensitive information unless a future DShario feature expressly supports that category with appropriate safeguards.

13. Privacy questions and complaints

For a privacy question, correction/deletion request or complaint, contact DShario through its official LinkedIn Page. A dedicated privacy email and formal request process should be established before commercial launch.

14. Applicable privacy requirements

DShario is being developed in Australia and may be used in other countries. Privacy obligations can depend on where DShario operates and where users are located. The policy and operational practices must be reviewed against applicable law before commercial launch in each target market.
